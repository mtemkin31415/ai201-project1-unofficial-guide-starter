Meet Your Dietitians
Here to help you navigate dining across campus

Have questions about nutrition, allergens or dietary restrictions while you are eating on campus?
Bearcat Dining is here to support you and ensure a safe and delicious dining experience if you have food allergies or medical conditions that require specific diets. We know dining is such an important part of campus life, and we want all students to have a successful dining experience. We believe in creating true partnerships: between you and our entire dining team - from our managers and chefs to our cooks and servers. We have many dining locations to help create community and foster healthy living and learning. If you are not finding dining options that meet your needs, contact us so we can help. You are the expert on YOU and your needs, and we look forward to working with you on a one-on-one basis if needed.

Alexa is passionate about helping students, faculty, and staff build healthy habits through fun and engaging nutrition and wellness experiences across campus. She works closely with the dining team and campus partners to create welcoming, inclusive dining environments while supporting students with food allergies, celiac disease, and other special dietary needs. In addition to providing nutrition consultations, wellness programs, and guest lectures, Alexa serves as an adjunct faculty member for the Health & Wellness Studies Department. She loves connecting with the campus community and sharing simple, practical ways to make healthy living feel enjoyable and achievable every day. 
Julie is a Registered Dietitian and Certified Intuitive Eating Counselor who has supported the Binghamton University campus community since 2016. She specializes in disordered eating, mental health, neurodivergence, and gastrointestinal disorders, and serves on the university's eating disorder management team. Passionate about education, Julie connects with the campus through both one-on-one student counseling and group workshops. She practices with a weight-inclusive philosophy, guiding individuals toward flexible, evidence-based nutrition and sustainable lifestyle habits that honor health independently of body size.
