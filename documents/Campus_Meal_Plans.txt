WILL I BE CHARGED AN ADMINISTRATIVE FEE ON MY MEAL PLAN FOR FALL 2026-27?
Bearcat Dining meal plans will not have an administrative fee charged to student accounts for this coming school year.

I WILL BE LIVING ON CAMPUS, AM I REQUIRED TO PURCHASE A MEAL PLAN?
All students living on campus are required to have a meal plan, except those living in Hillside and Susquehanna Apartments. Eligible students will be automatically enrolled in Plan B.

WHAT IS A DINING DOLLAR?
Dining Dollars are loaded directly to your Binghamton University ID card and are $1=1 Dining Dollars, and can be spent at any on-campus retail or residential dining location or market. The price of the item(s) purchased will be deducted from your Dining Dollars balance.

HOW DOES MY MEAL PLAN WORK?
Your Binghamton University ID card provides access to your meal plan. Your ID card will automatically keep track of your declining balance (DB).

The declining balance (DB) portion of your meal plan is like a prepaid debit account and is designed for use at all retail and residential dining locations and campus markets.

DO DINING DOLLARS ROLLOVER FROM FALL TO SPRING SEMESTER?
Yes, Dining Dollars will carry over semester to semester and year to year as long as you purchase a meal plan each consecutive semester. If you do not purchase a new meal plan consecutive semesters, you will lose your carryover. **Please note that carryover Dining Dollars will not be available until after the first week of classes for the semester.
