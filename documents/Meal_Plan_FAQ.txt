Meal Plan FAQ
GENERAL MEAL PLAN QUESTIONS
HOW DOES MY MEAL PLAN WORK?

Your Binghamton University ID card provides access to your meal plan and automatically keeps track of your Dining Dollar balance.

Dining Dollars work like a prepaid debit account and can be used at all residential dining and retail locations, campus markets, food trucks, and athletics concessions across campus.

WHAT IS A DINING DOLLAR?

Dining Dollars are loaded directly to your Binghamton University ID card and are accepted at all on-campus and satellite campus dining locations.

Dining Dollars are used at a $1= $1 Dining Dollar value toward food and beverage purchases. The original purchase price of your meal plan would be your starting balance available, plus any rollover dollars you may have from the previous semester, if applicable.

DO DINING DOLLARS ROLLOVER FROM FALL TO SPRING SEMESTER?

Yes, Dining Dollars will carry over semester to semester as long as you purchase a meal plan the next semester, prior to the add/drop date.

Eligible meal plans include:

- Residential Meal Plans

- Commuter Meal Plans

- Convenience Plan

If a student does not purchase a qualifying meal plan in a consecutive semester, remaining Dining Dollars will expire.

Please note:

- Carryover (rollover) Dining Dollars will become available after the first week of classes each semester.

- Students studying abroad may pause usage for one semester. Dining Dollars rollover upon return as long as a qualifying plan is purchased by the returning semester add/drop deadline.

HOW CAN I CHECK MY DINING DOLLAR BALANCE OR MAKE MEAL PLAN CHANGES?

Students can:

- View balances through the Grubhub mobile app

- Check receipts after purchases at campus dining locations


STUDENT MEAL PLAN QUESTIONS
ARE STUDENTS LIVING IN ON-CAMPUS RESIDENTIAL HOUSING REQUIRED TO PURCHASE A MEAL PLAN?

Yes. All students living on campus (Hinman, CIW, Newing & Dickinson) are required to participate in the meal plan program and will automatically be enrolled in Plan B. Students may change between eligible residential plans (Plans A-F) through the semester add/drop deadline - August 31, 2026 for the fall semester.

AM I REQUIRED TO PURCHASE A MEAL PLAN IF I LIVE IN THE ON-CAMPUS APARTMENTS?

Students living in Hillside Community or Susquehanna Community are exempt from the on-campus meal plan requirement, but are always welcome to take advantage of the benefits and savings by purchasing a meal plan, especially if you dine on campus.

I AM MOVING OFF CAMPUS. WILL I LOSE MY ROLLOVER DINING DOLLARS?

No. Beginning Summer 2026, students transitioning off campus may purchase any meal plan, including the Convenience Plan to maintain Dining Dollar rollover eligibility.

This applies to:

- Students moving off campus in Summer 2026 or Fall 2026

- Summer students remaining on campus

- Current commuter students

COMMUTER & CONVENIENCE PLAN QUESTIONS
WHAT IS THE CONVENIENCE PLAN?

The Convenience Plan is a flexible voluntary plan designed for commuter students, summer students, faculty/staff, and students transitioning off campus.

The plan allows eligible participants to:

- Add Dining Dollars for campus dining purchases

- Maintain Dining Dollar rollover eligibility

- Access all campus dining locations

IS THERE SALES TAX ON THE CONVENIENCE PLAN?

No sales tax is charged when purchasing the Convenience Plan itself.

However:

- Students remain tax exempt when using Dining Dollars

- Faculty and staff members are subject to applicable sales tax at the register.

WILL THERE BE A MEAL PLAN OPTION AVAILABLE THIS SUMMER?

Yes. Students enrolled in summer courses or remaining on campus may purchase the convenience plan during the summer term.


FACULTY & STAFF QUESTIONS
WHAT IS THE BENEFIT TO FACULTY/STAFF AND DEPARTMENT MEAL PLANS?

Faculty/Staff and Department Accounts are eligible for a 10% bonus when Dining Dollars are purchased in $100 increments.

WILL FACULTY/STAFF AND DEPARTMENT ACCOUNTS EXPIRE?

No, Faculty/Staff Dining Dollars will automatically rollover from semester to semester with no expiration, as long as the faculty/staff is employed by Binghamton University.
